Key Features

Contact Upload & Broadcast

Upload CSV contacts and send bulk messages to thousands of recipients. Track delivery, read receipts, and campaign performance in real time.

\ud83d\udcac

Two-way Inbox

Receive and reply to messages in a live conversation view. Manage the 24-hour service window, send media, and keep your customers engaged.

\ud83c\udfa8

Template Builder

Design rich message templates with media headers, coupon codes, carousels, and interactive buttons (call, URL, quick reply). Preview before sending.

\ud83d\udcc5

Recurring Campaigns

Schedule campaigns to run on a recurring basis. Set it and forget it — automated messaging on your schedule with pause/resume controls.

Who Is It For?

  • Small businesses managing their own WhatsApp
  • Developers who want full control over their messaging stack
  • Anyone who wants a free alternative to paid WhatsApp APIs
  • Teams that need data sovereignty and self-hosting

Get Started in 5 Minutes

git clone https://github.com/Ibrahim-Abedrabboh/whatsapp-hub-free.git
cd whatsapp-hub-free
cp .env.example .env
docker compose up --build

That's it! The app is running at http://localhost:3000
