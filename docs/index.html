Features

Everything you need to message at scale

Four powerful modules that cover your entire WhatsApp messaging workflow.

Contact Upload & Broadcast

Upload CSV contacts and send bulk messages to thousands of recipients. Track delivery, read receipts, and campaign performance in real time.

Two-way Inbox

Receive and reply to messages in a live conversation view. Manage the 24-hour service window, send media, and keep your customers engaged.

Template Builder

Design rich message templates with media headers, coupon codes, carousels, and interactive buttons. Preview before submitting to Meta.

Recurring Campaigns

Schedule campaigns to run on a recurring basis. Set it and forget it — automated messaging on your schedule with pause/resume controls.

Quick Start

Up and running in 5 minutes

Clone, configure, and launch with Docker. That's the whole setup.

Terminal
$git clone https://github.com/al-ai-hq/whatsapp-hub-free-version.git
$cd whatsapp-hub-free-version
$cp .env.example .env
$docker compose up --build

That's it! App running at localhost:3000
